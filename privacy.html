Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

content-poster is a private tool that publishes content to social media accounts owned by its operator. It is not offered to the public.

What data we access

  • Basic profile information of the connected account (account ID, display name, username), used only to confirm the right account is connected.
  • Access tokens issued by the platform (TikTok, Facebook, Instagram) after the account owner authorizes the tool.

How data is used and stored

  • Access tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer, in files readable only by the operator. They are not stored on any server or shared.
  • Tokens are used only to upload content the operator created to the operator's own accounts.
  • We do not collect data about other people, do not sell or share data, and do not use data for advertising.

Removing access

The account owner can revoke access at any time from the platform's settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions). Revoking access makes stored tokens invalid. Local token files can be deleted at any time.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised at github.com/apgame29/content-poster/issues.
