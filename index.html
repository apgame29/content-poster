content-poster

A private publishing tool for the owner's own social media accounts.

content-poster is an internal tool used by a single owner to publish content to the owner's own accounts: the woowey and Game Changer brand pages on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.

How it works

  1. The owner prepares a post or video on their own computer.
  2. The owner reviews and approves it.
  3. The tool uploads it to the owner's account. On TikTok, videos are sent to the account's drafts inbox, and the owner finishes and publishes the post inside the TikTok app.

The tool is not offered to the public, does not have user sign-up, and only connects accounts that the owner controls.
