Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

content-poster is a private tool that publishes content to social media accounts owned by its operator (an individual creator). It is not offered to the public and has no users other than the operator.

Use of YouTube API Services

content-poster uses YouTube API Services to upload the operator's own videos to the operator's own YouTube channels and to set their title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions and publish time.

By using content-poster with YouTube you are also subject to the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and agree to the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms).

What information we access

  • YouTube: the connected channel's ID and title (via channels.list, to confirm the right channel is connected), and the videos the operator uploads or edits through the tool.
  • TikTok, Facebook, Instagram: basic account information (account ID, display name) of the operator's own accounts.
  • Access tokens issued by Google, TikTok and Meta after the account owner authorizes the tool.

We do not access, collect or analyze any information about other YouTube users or other channels.

How information is used, stored and shared

  • Information is used only to publish and update the operator's own content on the operator's own accounts.
  • Access and refresh tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer, in files readable only by the operator. Nothing is stored on any server operated by us.
  • We do not sell, share or disclose any information to third parties, and do not use it for advertising.
  • content-poster does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies, and does not place any on users' devices.

Revoking access

  • Google / YouTube: revoke content-poster's access at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
  • Facebook / Instagram: Settings → Business integrations (Facebook) or Apps and websites (Instagram).

Data retention and deletion

Stored tokens are kept only while the account remains connected. When access is revoked, or on request, the local token files are deleted within 7 days. Because no other data is stored, there is nothing else to delete. Deletion requests can be made at the contact below.
